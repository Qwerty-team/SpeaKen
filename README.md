### Team:Qwerty 
### SpeaKen - an interactive web platform designed to help language learners conquer the speaking section of exams like IELTS and TOEFL through timed, spontaneous presentations. The system generates a random topic, gives users 15 minutes to research and organize their ideas, and records their speech via camera and microphone. Integrated automated feedback evaluates fluency, grammar, vocabulary, and delivery structure to build confidence and real-world public speaking skills.
